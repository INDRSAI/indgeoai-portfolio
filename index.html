Profile

From hydraulic survey maps to deep-learning segmentation models.

I lead the delivery of GIS, remote sensing, and geospatial AI projects for clients across North America, Australia, Europe, and the Middle East — spanning environmental monitoring, utility GIS, and natural resource sectors.

My work sits at the intersection of enterprise geospatial systems and applied machine learning — building production-ready models for land-cover classification, wetland mapping, terrain-based gully detection, and OCR-driven feature extraction, then deploying them into real field workflows.
